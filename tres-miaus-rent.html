Which rate applies to me?

Community vs commercial

Community rate

  • Yoga, meditation, and wellness classes
  • Personal celebrations — birthdays, farewells, dinners
  • Community meetups, language groups, hobby circles
  • Neptunes events and Tres Miaus programming
  • Independent artists and small creative showcases
  • NGOs and non-profit gatherings
  • Events generating less than €300 in ticket revenue

When in doubt: if you're not making significant money from the event, you're community rate.

Commercial rate

  • Company team events, offsites, and workshops
  • Brand activations and product launches
  • Professional content creation and paid shoots
  • High-ticket workshops (€50+ per person)
  • Corporate bookings of any size
  • Events generating €300+ in ticket revenue

Still a great deal compared to the Barcelona market. Commercial bookings help keep community rates accessible for everyone.

Questions

Things people ask

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Yes — you're welcome to bring food and drinks for your event. The kitchenette has basic prep space and fridge access. We just ask that you leave the space clean.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancellations more than 48 hours before your booking receive a full refund. Within 48 hours, 50% of the booking fee is retained. We'll always try to rebook the slot if possible.

Can I play music during my event?

Yes — the Bluetooth speaker is yours to use. We ask that you're considerate of neighbours, especially for evening events. We don't host DJ or amplified dance events.

Are the cats always in the space?

Usually yes — Yuki, Thelma, and Louise live here. If you or a guest has allergies, let us know in advance and we'll do our best to manage the space accordingly.

Is there outdoor space included?

Yes — the terrace is included with all bookings, weather permitting. It's a plant-filled sun-trap and a favourite spot of Louise's, so you may have company.

Do you offer recurring bookings?

Yes — if you need the space weekly or monthly, ask about our Residente membership tier, which includes hours and priority booking at a reduced rate.
