Meet the team

The three bosses

🐱

Yuki

Independent spirit & selective bestie

Grey and white. Gloriously fluffy. Does entirely her own thing — unless she likes you, in which case she'll silently appear beside you. If Yuki sits near you, consider it the highest honour.

🐈

Thelma

Resident beauty, keeping her distance

Brown ombré coat, blue eyes, absolutely stunning — and she knows it. Thelma prefers to admire gatherings from afar. Please don't approach or touch her; she'll come to you if and when she's ready.

🐈‍⬛

Louise

Chief of chaos & head of entertainment

Black, green-eyed, and absolutely feral in the best way. Louise will meow until you acknowledge her, play until you're tired, and demand pets on her own schedule — which is always. The life of every event.
