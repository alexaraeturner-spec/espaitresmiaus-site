The founder

Hi, I'm Alexa

I'm an aspiring herbalist, witch, and maker — drawn to the intersection of plant medicine, magic, and craft. I make and sell herbal creations, magical and medicinal, and I've spent years building a practice around the plants, rituals, and community that make life feel more rooted.

Tres Miaus is the physical home for all of that. A space where I live, practice, and host — and where other people can bring their own work, their own community, their own rituals.

"I wanted a space that felt like something — warm, alive, full of plants and cats and the kind of people who make things and gather on purpose."

The three cats were non-negotiable. Yuki, Thelma, and Louise have been part of the practice longer than the space has existed. They'll be here when you arrive, and probably watching when you leave.

The practice behind the space

Neptune's Herbal Lab

Before Tres Miaus, there was Neptune's Herbal Lab — a community practice built around herbalism, magic, and arts and crafts. Events, workshops, and handmade herbal creations, all rooted in the belief that plant medicine and ritual belong in everyday life.

Tres Miaus is where Neptune's Herbal Lab found its permanent home. The space was built to host the kind of events that Neptune's has always run — intimate, hands-on, grounded in the natural world — and to offer that same space to others who want to gather with intention.

The residents

Yuki, Thelma & Louise

🐱 British shorthair · grey & white

Yuki

Independent spirit & selective bestie

Gloriously fluffy and entirely self-directed — Yuki does exactly what she wants, when she wants, at whatever pace suits her. She won't come to you. But if she likes you, she'll simply appear beside you one day, without announcement.

✦ If Yuki sits near you, you've passed a test you didn't know you were taking.
🐈 Brown ombré · blue eyes

Thelma

Resident beauty, keeping her distance

Thelma is stunning — brown ombré coat, blue eyes, the kind of cat you can't help but stare at. She observes gatherings from a safe distance and has no interest in being approached. She's been through things. She deserves respect.

⚠ Please do not approach or try to touch Thelma. She will come to you if and when she's ready — and that's entirely her call.
🐈‍⬛ Black · green eyes

Louise

Chief of chaos & head of entertainment

Louise is black, green-eyed, and absolutely feral in the best possible way. She will meow at you until you pay attention. She will play until you give up. She loves to be petted, loves her back scratched, and will absolutely make herself the centre of your event.

✦ Louise is available for pats, play, and general chaos at all times. She will let you know.
